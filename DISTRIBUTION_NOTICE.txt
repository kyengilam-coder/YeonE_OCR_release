Unicode Unihan 17.0.0 source-preservation data is bundled in
yeone_ocr/data/unihan_source_protection_17_0_0.json. It includes all relationships
from the selected compatibility, semantic, specialized semantic, simplified,
traditional and Z-variant properties, plus official Mandarin readings. The data
is used to hold risky new OCR proposals, never to normalize or convert a text.
